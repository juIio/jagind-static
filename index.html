What We Buy

A Dependable Outlet for Your Bulk Inventory

Bulk Pallet Purchasing

We buy full pallets and truckloads of new merchandise directly from brands, manufacturers, and large retailers. One buyer, one transaction, no piecemeal selling.

  • Pallet and truckload quantities
  • Mixed and single-SKU loads
  • Quick, firm purchase offers
  • Reliable, on-time payment

Apparel & Footwear

Clothing and shoes are our core focus. We know how to move seasonal styles, size runs, and category assortments through retail.

  • Men's, women's, and kids' apparel

  • Seasonal and prior-season styles
  • Full and broken size runs

Excess & Overstock Inventory

Overproduction, canceled orders, and slow-moving stock tie up capital and warehouse space. We turn that inventory into cash quickly and discreetly.

  • Overstock and surplus goods
  • Canceled and refused orders
  • End-of-season closeouts
  • Discontinued product lines

Brand-Safe Retail Placement

Your brand reputation matters. We place product in physical retail locations and work with you on channel and pricing guidelines so your goods are presented the right way.

  • Brick-and-mortar retail placement
  • Respect for channel restrictions
  • Pricing guidelines honored
  • Confidential transactions
How We Work

Our Simple 4-Step Process

From first conversation to inventory off your books

1

Share Your Inventory

Send us a manifest or a quick description of what you have: categories, quantities, condition, and location.

2

Receive an Offer

We review the load and come back with a clear, competitive purchase offer, typically within 48 hours.

3

Pickup & Payment

Once agreed, we coordinate freight and pickup from your warehouse and pay on the terms we set together.

4

Retail Sell-Through

We place your product in our retail locations, following any brand guidelines, and come back for the next load.
