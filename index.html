What we buy

New merchandise in volume. If it's taking up warehouse space or tying up capital, we want to see it.

Apparel

Our core category. We move seasonal styles, full assortments, and broken size runs through retail.

  • Men's, women's, and kids'
  • Denim, activewear, outerwear
  • Current and prior season
  • Full and broken size runs

Footwear

Cased or loose pairs, single styles or mixed loads.

  • Athletic and casual
  • Boots and sandals
  • Mixed sizes and colorways
  • Boxed and unboxed pairs

Overstock and closeouts

Overproduction, canceled orders, and slow-moving stock, turned into cash quickly and quietly.

  • Surplus production
  • Canceled and refused orders
  • End-of-season closeouts
  • Discontinued lines

Consumer goods

Accessories and general merchandise that sell alongside apparel in a retail setting.

  • Bags and accessories
  • Home and lifestyle goods
  • Seasonal merchandise
  • Single-SKU or mixed pallets

How it works

  1. 1

    Send your inventory

    Share a manifest or a short description: categories, quantities, condition, and where it's stored.

  2. 2

    Get an offer

    We review the load and send a clear purchase offer, usually within 48 hours.

  3. 3

    We pick it up

    Once you accept, we schedule freight from your dock and pay on the terms we agreed.

  4. 4

    It sells at retail

    Your product goes to retail locations, following your guidelines. Then we're ready for the next load.

Why sell to us

Decisions you can plan around

We answer quickly and stand behind our offers, so the sale you schedule is the sale that closes.

One buyer for the whole load

Clear entire pallets and truckloads in a single transaction instead of splitting stock across small buyers.

Physical retail, not discount sites

Your product reaches real customers in stores, not online listings that undercut your main channels.

Your brand rules, respected

We follow channel restrictions, pricing guidelines, and confidentiality agreements as part of every deal.

Logistics handled

We arrange freight and pickup, so moving inventory takes very little of your team's time.

One person to call

You work with a single contact who knows your product and schedule, which keeps repeat loads simple.
